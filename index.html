Our vision

Tracking money isn’t about logging every penny. It’s about seeing how much freedom you have. Latitude doesn’t want you worrying about everything you spend. It wants you to know how much room you still have to choose.

Clarity over complexity. Freedom over restriction. Calm over pressure.

Room Score

A 0–100 score built from five dimensions: liquidity, cash flow, resilience, obligations and flexibility. It always explains why it is what it is.

Log in seconds

Add an expense or income with a category, payment method and note in a few taps.

Screenshot entry

After you pay, ask Siri. Latitude reads the amount and merchant from the payment screen and logs it for you.

Statistics

Monthly and yearly spending trends and category breakdowns.

Recurring items

Salary, rent and subscriptions are logged automatically when they’re due.

Private by design

Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no server, no tracking. Face ID lock is built in.
