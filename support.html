Support

Need help? Email latitude.app.support@icloud.com. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I set up screenshot entry?

Go to Me → Screenshot Entry and tap Add “Log Payment” in One Tap, then tap “Add Shortcut” in the Shortcuts app. Or open How to Set Up and build it yourself: in the Shortcuts app, add “Get Current App”, “Take Screenshot” and Latitude’s “Record Payment Screenshot” action, then name the shortcut “Log Payment”. After paying, stay on the payment confirmation screen and say “Log Payment” to Siri, or assign the shortcut to the Action button.

How is the Room Score calculated?

It combines five dimensions (liquidity, cash flow, resilience, obligations and flexibility) using fixed rules on your iPhone. Tap the score and then “Why this score?” to see every step. The more complete your financial profile and entries, the higher the confidence.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you use iCloud Backup or Quick Start to transfer your iPhone. Latitude has no account or sync server, so your data travels only inside your own device backup. You can also export a CSV at any time.

How do I delete my data?

Me → Privacy & Security → Delete All Data. This cannot be undone. Deleting the app also removes all its data.

The app says it can’t read its data

Your entries are still there. Unlock your iPhone, or restart it and open Latitude again. If it still happens, check that your iPhone has free storage space, then contact us.
