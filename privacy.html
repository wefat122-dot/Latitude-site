Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Latitude is a budget tracker and financial “Room Score” app for iPhone. We built it so that your financial information never leaves your device. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Data we collect

None. Latitude has no account, no server and no analytics. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data, usage data or device identifiers. The app contains no advertising and no third-party tracking code.

Data stored on your device

The entries, categories, payment methods, financial profile, recurring items and Room Score history you create are stored only on your iPhone, in the app’s private storage, protected by iOS data protection. If you use iCloud Backup or transfer to a new iPhone, iOS may include this data in your device backup under your own Apple Account, as with any other app. We cannot access it.

Network access

Latitude does not send any of your data over the internet. Tapping a link in the app (such as a shortcut link) opens it in Apple’s Shortcuts app or Safari.

Screenshot entry

When you use the “Record Payment Screenshot” shortcut action, the screenshot is processed on your iPhone with Apple’s on-device text recognition to read the amount and merchant. An on-device language model (Apple Intelligence, or a small Qwen model bundled in the app in some regions) may pick a category. The screenshot is used only for that entry. It is not saved to your photo library or anywhere else, and it is never uploaded.

Face ID

If you turn on Face ID Lock, authentication is handled entirely by iOS. Latitude never receives your face data or passcode.

Your control

  • Export all entries as a CSV file at any time (Me → Data).
  • Permanently delete all data at any time (Me → Privacy & Security → Delete All Data), or by deleting the app.

Not financial advice

The Room Score and its explanations help you understand your own finances. They are not investment, financial, tax or lending advice.

Children

Latitude is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If Latitude ever needs to connect to the internet for a new feature, this policy will say so before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: latitude.app.support@icloud.com
